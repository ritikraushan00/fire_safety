About The Business

Family-run, Mohali-based, on call since 2019

Incepted in 2019, Falcon Fire System is a trader and retailer of fire pumps, extinguishers, hydrant valves, hose pipes and sprinkler components — sourced from established brands like Lubi, Kirloskar, New Guard and Omex.

Who We Are

Equipping sites across the tricity, one enquiry at a time

Falcon Fire System operates out of Mohali, Punjab, supplying fire safety equipment to industrial units, housing societies, offices and commercial buildings. Every enquiry — whether it's a single extinguisher refill or a multi-pump installation — goes directly to owner M Tyagi.

Our catalogue spans eight equipment families: fire pumps, fire extinguishers, hose pipes, trolley-mounted units, landing valves, sprinklers, pipe nozzles and hose boxes, drawing on trusted manufacturers so that whatever leaves our stock meets the fire rating a site actually needs.
